## What it is

Obsidian plugin for language-learning spaced repetition (forked from [Obsidian Better Recall](https://github.com/FlorianWoelki/obsidian-better-recall)). TypeScript — **not React** (first non-React project in recent times for Chas).

## Why it matters (Chas's words)

- **Daily driver** — uses it every day.
- **AI interface is the killer feature** — can CRUD cards on its own; excellent learning experience. Often gets clarity on a complex Spanish concept, turns it into a card, never forgets it.
- **Bulk card generation** — nouns/verbs from life context (TV, travel, handy-man tools/tasks) instead of manual entry or downloading generic decks. Better than studying words encountered in the wild.
- **Fluid UI** — cool and fluid interface; still a work in progress.

## Tech / features (from repo)

- Decks, SRS review (Anki algorithm), built-in translation (no API key for front→back)
- AI follow-up tutor: Gemini + Groq/OpenRouter fallback; system prompt tuning
- Obsidian-native views (Decks, Review, Card editor, Followup chat)
